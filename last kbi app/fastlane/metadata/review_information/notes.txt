KBI Technician is a workforce app for approved KBI field technicians. Use the supplied review account, which is approved and contains demo jobs.

Demo path: Sign in → Orders → open the demo order → Closeout → Official Invoice → Share.

Account deletion: Profile → Settings → Delete Account Permanently. Enter the review-account password to confirm.

Background location is used only while the technician is online/on duty or handling an active service. Going Offline stops tracking. A prominent disclosure appears before the system permission prompt.
